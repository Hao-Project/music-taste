Mood map of my library

Valence runs from sad (−1) to happy (+1), arousal from calm (−1) to energetic (+1). Both maps use LLM (Claude) ratings only, so the scale is consistent. There are two separate ratings that are never mixed: track mood rates a song the LLM knows well, and songs it doesn't know are left out; artist mood rates each artist's general sound. Last.fm is used only in the robustness check at the end.

Robustness check: LLM vs Last.fm

104 songs have Last.fm mood tags; 90 of them also have an LLM track rating and were rated blind to their tags (5 songs whose tags I saw beforehand are excluded). The LLM artist rating is shown as a baseline: if the song-level rating is worth having, it should agree with Last.fm better than the artist's general mood does.

How to read this. Agreement with Last.fm is not the same as accuracy: Last.fm scores here often rest on one or two tags ("ethereal", "cute"), so they are noisy too. The overlap is also not a random sample. Songs with Last.fm mood tags are the more popular ones, which are also the songs the LLM knows best, so agreement here likely overstates how well the LLM does on the niche songs in the library.
